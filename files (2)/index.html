About us

Blue Island Travels is a travel and tourism service provider established in January 2021 in Doha, Qatar. We are committed to delivering reliable, professional, and customer-focused travel solutions to individuals, families, and corporate clients.

With our main office at Plaza Mall, Asian Town, and branches in Al Attiya and Al Wakrah, we aim to make travel services accessible and convenient for our customers.

Our mission is to simplify travel by providing comprehensive travel assistance, visa services, flight reservations, accommodation arrangements, and customized travel solutions.

Our vision

To become a trusted and recognized travel and tourism service provider in Qatar by delivering quality services, building lasting customer relationships, and continuously improving our travel solutions.

Our mission

  • To provide reliable and affordable travel services.
  • To ensure customer satisfaction through professional assistance.
  • To make travel planning simple, convenient, and stress-free.
  • To build long-term relationships based on trust and transparency.
  • To provide efficient visa and travel support.
